problem:
  kicker: The problem
  title: Classroom training doesn't scale. E-learning doesn't stick.
  paragraphs:
    - A great instructor reads the room. They notice who already knows the material, adjust the examples to the audience and put people to work on real problems together. Most online training does none of this. Everyone gets the same slides, the same quiz and the same certificate, whether they're a CFO or a system administrator.
    - The result is predictable. Large field studies find little measurable effect from annual click-through security modules, and open online courses are typically finished by only a small share of those who start. **Sparkurity was built to close that gap.**

steps:
  kicker: How it works
  title: Diagnose, adapt, practise, prove.
  items:
    - title: Diagnose
      text: Learners create a short profile (field of work, role, experience) and take an initial skill assessment. The platform builds a picture of what each person already knows, module by module.
    - title: Adapt
      text: An AI-generated personal roadmap marks each module *skip*, *review carefully*, *standard pace* or *focus area*. Exercises move from worked examples to independent work as competence grows. The instructor reviews and approves every recommendation.
    - title: Practise together
      text: In cohort courses, participants work on realistic cases and role-play scenarios, such as auditor and auditee or incident lead and communications. Each role sees the information that role would really have.
    - title: Prove mastery
      text: Short retrieval checks, gate quizzes and AI-assisted written-answer grading confirm understanding before learners move on. If someone struggles, their roadmap updates automatically.
